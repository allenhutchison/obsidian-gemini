### [#1681 Enable thinking for all current Gemini models (#1678)](https://github.com/allenhutchison/obsidian-gemini/pull/1681)

`supportsThinking()` used to allowlist `gemini-2.5` and `gemini-3`, so `gemini-4-argon` and the version-less `-latest` aliases were sent without thinking settings and showed no reasoning in the agent view. It now denylists the legacy generations that predate thinking instead. Because some models reject certain `thinking_level` values (for example `minimal`, used by completions), `createInteraction` retries a thinking-level 400 once without the level and remembers the rejected model and level pair for the life of the client. The Model Reasoning section of `docs/guide/agent-mode.md` was updated to match.
