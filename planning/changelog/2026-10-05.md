### [#1629 refactor(commands): share the agent-view command entry gate](https://github.com/allenhutchison/obsidian-gemini/pull/1629)

Five agent-view commands (`switch-project`, `browse-sessions`, `link-project`, `session-settings`, `toggle-plan-mode`) carried the same five-line initialization gate. An `addAgentViewCommand()` helper in `register-commands.ts` now owns it, so a change to the gate lands in all five at once.

`FailurePauseTracker.recordSuccess` and `.reset` built the same cleared-failure record by hand. A private `clearedState()` helper now produces it for both, with no behavior change.
