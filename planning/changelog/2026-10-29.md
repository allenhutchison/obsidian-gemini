## What shipped

### [#1711 Share generateContent text collection in one Gemini leaf (#1700)](https://github.com/allenhutchison/obsidian-gemini/pull/1711)

Two places gathered text from a Gemini `generateContent` response with separate loops that had drifted apart. They now share `collectResponseText()` in `src/api/providers/gemini/response-text.ts`, used by `web_fetch` and the grounding tool runner (`google_search`, `google_maps`). The dead `result.text` fallback is removed.

This is not a pure refactor: the shared helper skips thought parts, so model reasoning no longer ends up in what those tools return.
