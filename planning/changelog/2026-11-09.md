### [#1742 Remove three public class members with no caller](https://github.com/allenhutchison/obsidian-gemini/pull/1742)

`knip` resolves exported symbols, not class members, so a public method or getter on a live class can lose its last caller and stay green. The architecture sweep found three: `AgentViewSend.getTurnToolCallCount()`, `ProjectManager.updateProjectConfig()` and the `BackgroundStatusBar.pendingCatchUpCount` getter. All three are removed; the backing fields remain in use inside their own classes, so there is no behavior change.
